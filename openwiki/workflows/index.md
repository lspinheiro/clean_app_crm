# Files

- [Cleaner App Join and Open-Jobs Board](cleaner-app.md) - The cleaner Next.js app is a client-first workspace application for invitation-based pool joining and a privacy-minimized board of open job slots across a cleaner's active pools.
- [Client and Site CSV Import](client-site-import.md) - Company administrators can preview and independently import client or site CSV rows. The browser parser rejects malformed, invalid, ambiguous, and duplicate rows before each ready row delegates to the existing protected create actions.
- [Bilingual CRM Routing and Locale Preference](crm-localization.md) - The CRM ships Australian English and Brazilian Portuguese through one locale-owned App Router tree. It detects or selects a locale in the URL, persists an authenticated user's preference through a self-scoped RPC, and preserves Australian operating rules and user-authored content.
- [One-Off Job Creation and Crew-Slot Dispatch](job-dispatch.md) - The implemented job workflow creates draft or posted one-off jobs through an RPC, derives slot state from assignment history, assigns an eligible cleaner to a numbered slot, and refreshes job and roster consumers after mutations.
- [Company Pay Ledger](pay-ledger.md) - The read-only CRM Money page projects agreed cleaner pay from immutable database ledger entries. Completion creates one owed entry per active assignment; settlement is a database-controlled owed-to-paid transition and records a notification without moving money.
